# 📱 Device Compatibility

## ✅ Fully Supported (Tested)
| Brand       | Model               | Method               | Notes                          |
|-------------|---------------------|----------------------|--------------------------------|
| Samsung     | Galaxy S8-S23       | ADB, Fastboot, APK   | Works on most Exynos/Snapdragon|
| Samsung     | Galaxy A Series     | ADB, APK             | May require OEM unlock         |
| Xiaomi      | Redmi Note Series   | Fastboot, APK        | Mi Account removal works       |
| Google      | Pixel 3-8           | ADB, Fastboot        | Requires unlocked bootloader   |
| OnePlus     | 6-11                | Fastboot             | OEM unlock required            |

## ⚠️ Partially Supported
| Brand       | Model               | Method               | Notes                          |
|-------------|---------------------|----------------------|--------------------------------|
| Huawei      | P30, Mate Series    | APK Injection         | EMUI restrictions apply        |
| Oppo/Realme | Any                 | ADB (if enabled)      | ColorOS may block methods      |
| Motorola    | G Series            | Fastboot             | Some models work               |

## ❌ Not Supported
| Brand       | Model               | Reason                          |
|-------------|---------------------|---------------------------------|
| iPhone      | All                 | iOS does not have FRP           |
| Huawei      | Newer Models        | No known bypass methods         |
| LG          | Most Models         | Bootloader locked by default    |
